# Licensing and attribution

The repository's root licence governs its original code. Check individual assets and dependencies for their own terms.

## Reused design guidance

GOV.UK, MoJ, DWP, Home Office and ONS guidance informs this local system. Their documentation is generally published under the Open Government Licence where stated. NHS material has its own published terms. Links in this site identify the specific sources; attribution does not imply endorsement.

The GOV.UK Tech Docs Template and its code are MIT licensed. Its documentation identifies separate Crown copyright/Open Government Licence treatment. Preserve dependency notices and the repository's existing attribution.

Standalone chart examples include Apache ECharts, zrender and their supporting code. Their [licences and notices](../reference/chart-demo-licenses.txt) accompany the examples. The Home Assistant companion uses the native renderer and does not distribute another production chart engine. ONS visual specifications are adapted through original local code; the ONS Highcharts implementation is not included.

## Brand assets

The civica.uk V3.2 files were supplied by the user. Their provenance and mechanical verification records are retained in `brand/civica/`. Do not infer a new licence or legal clearance from the presence of those files. The included source artwork remains unchanged.

The civica.uk mark is an independent identity. It is not an official GOV.UK or Home Assistant mark. Do not distribute the GOV.UK crown, GOV.UK logotype or GDS Transport as product branding.

## Screenshots and data

Use synthetic or authorised data in public examples. Do not publish private device names, location data, camera images or tokens incidentally captured in a screenshot.

## Attribution wording

“civica.uk adapts guidance from the GOV.UK Design System and other UK public service design systems for Home Assistant. It is independent and is not endorsed by those organisations.”
