# Patterns

Patterns combine components into complete household tasks. They describe the user need and expected behaviour, not just a screenshot.

- [Home and rooms](home.html): scan the home and find a room or device.
- [Energy and history](energy.html): understand use and trends without losing native functions.
- [Settings and preferences](settings.html): make a reversible personal choice.
- [Errors and recovery](recovery.html): distinguish empty, unavailable and failed states.

## Write a pattern before adding a special case

Identify who needs it, what they are trying to do and why existing components are insufficient. Describe entry, normal completion, failure and cancellation. Record the source pattern and any HA-specific adaptation.

Use the [controlled deviation register](../community/deviations.html) to keep adaptations deliberate. A new component should not introduce another department's branding or an isolated colour scheme.
